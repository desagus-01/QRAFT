# Changelog

All notable changes to QRAFT will be documented in this file.

## 0.1.0a1

Initial public alpha release.

### Included

- Entropy pooling and flexible market views.
- Forecast generation and scenario simulation.
- Single-period and multi-period portfolio construction.
- Backtesting and execution-cost accounting.
- Walk-forward and combinatorial cross-validation.
- Portfolio risk measurement and attribution.

### Alpha limitations

- Public APIs may change before the 1.0 release.
- Results require independent validation before production use.
- The package is intended for research and educational use, not financial or
  investment advice.
- Known limitations and defects are tracked in the project issue tracker.
